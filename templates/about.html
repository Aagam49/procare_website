About us

Community health and rehabilitation with a personal approach.

At ProCare Physiotherapy, we work with people who want to move comfortably, recover safely, and live with more confidence. Our clinic supports pain relief, mobility, functional strength, and long-term wellness in Naranpura, Ahmedabad.

Whether you are managing postural strain, sports injury, chronic pain, or difficulty with daily movement, we focus on practical treatment plans that are easy to follow and tailored to your real-life routine.

Meet the lead physio

Dr Vishva Shah (MPT)

Dr Vishva Shah is a physiotherapy professional focused on improving mobility, relieving pain, and restoring confidence in everyday movement. Her clinical style blends hands-on therapy, structured exercise programs, and patient education.

With a strong interest in community health and rehabilitation, she works closely with patients to build practical solutions for recovery, prevention, and long-term wellness.
